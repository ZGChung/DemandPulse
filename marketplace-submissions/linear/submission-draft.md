## Headline

Capture product demand signals and connect them to Linear execution.

## Overview

DemandPulse helps teams collect validated product requirements from developer conversations and turn them into structured demand signals. A Linear integration would let product and engineering teams connect those signals to the issues, projects, and planning work already tracked in Linear.

## How it works

DemandPulse captures requirement-style feedback from developer tooling, deduplicates repeated pain points, and surfaces trend data across bugs, missing integrations, workflow friction, and feature demand.

With a Linear integration, teams would connect a workspace through OAuth, review matched demand signals in DemandPulse, and link high-signal trends back to Linear issues or projects for prioritization. This creates a path from raw developer feedback to execution without relying on ad hoc spreadsheets or manual copy-paste.

## To configure

1. A Linear workspace admin clicks Connect Linear from the DemandPulse integrations page.
2. The admin authorizes the DemandPulse OAuth app in Linear.
3. DemandPulse loads the workspace teams, issues, and project references needed for linking.
4. Product or engineering leads review demand clusters in DemandPulse and connect them to the relevant Linear records.

## Core value points

- Converts scattered developer feedback into trackable requirement trends.
- Gives product teams evidence for prioritization decisions.
- Connects emerging demand back to Linear execution artifacts.
- Reduces manual triage between feedback collection and roadmap planning.
