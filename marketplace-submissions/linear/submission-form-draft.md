## Overview

DemandPulse helps product and engineering teams understand what developers are trying to build right now. It collects sanitized requirement signals from AI-assisted development workflows, clusters them into trends, and turns repeated demand into action-ready insights for planning.

## How it works

DemandPulse captures explicit requirement submissions from AI coding sessions, removes sensitive details, and analyzes the underlying jobs-to-be-done across teams and projects. The Linear integration would let teams push validated signals into Linear as issues, initiatives, or triage inputs, so repeated customer and developer pain points become visible inside the planning system where work already happens.

## Configure

1. Connect DemandPulse to a Linear workspace with OAuth.
2. Choose which requirement types should sync to Linear, such as feature requests, workflow friction, missing integrations, or repeated bug reports.
3. Configure routing rules for team, project, labels, or priority.
4. Review clustered demand in DemandPulse and send high-confidence signals into Linear for follow-up.
