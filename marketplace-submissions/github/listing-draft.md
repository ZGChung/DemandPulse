## Introductory description

Capture developer feedback from your GitHub workflow, detect recurring demand signals, and turn issue noise into structured product insight for roadmap and prioritization decisions.

## Detailed description

DemandPulse helps teams understand what developers are repeatedly asking for across their tooling and delivery workflow. A future GitHub App would let teams connect repositories, issues, or discussions to DemandPulse so product and engineering leads can spot recurring bugs, feature requests, and workflow friction earlier.

Instead of relying on scattered issue labels or ad hoc manual triage, teams would use DemandPulse to aggregate demand signals, review trend data, and connect high-signal findings to planning decisions.

## Proposed feature bullets

- Aggregate issue and discussion signals into recurring demand themes
- Detect repeated requests, bugs, and workflow pain points across repositories
- Give product teams a trend view instead of one-off anecdotal feedback
- Connect GitHub-originated signals to DemandPulse analysis and reporting
